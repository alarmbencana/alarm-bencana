Subject: Notice of non-profit public use of the WAQI API - Alarm Bencana (Indonesia)

Hello WAQI team,

As your API terms ask, I am letting you know before we start public, non-profit use of the API.

Project: Alarm Bencana (@AlarmBencana on X), a free automated account that warns people in Indonesia about forest fire smoke during the fire season. It has no ads, no paid features, and no income of any kind.

How we use the data:
- PM2.5 readings from Indonesian stations (BMKG and KLH) through the token registered to this email address.
- A small program checks the API at most once an hour (about 130 requests per check) and posts a short alert with a map image when a station reaches unhealthy levels.
- We do not store, archive, or redistribute the raw data.

Attribution: every map image credits the World Air Quality Index Project (aqicn.org) together with the original station owners, BMKG and KLH. Text posts credit "WAQI".

If you have any concerns, or would like us to do something differently, please reply and we will follow your guidance.

Thank you for making this data available.
